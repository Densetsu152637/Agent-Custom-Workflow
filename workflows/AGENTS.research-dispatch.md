# Research dispatch policy

Manager-only: load before selecting a research assignment's ceiling, model, or effort. Workers need only the methods core.

## Specialized capability ceiling

Generic [ceiling](AGENTS.manager.md#capability-ceilings) and [selection](AGENTS.manager.md#model-and-effort-selection) rules apply except:

- Substantive research of any difficulty (lookup, verification, comparison, analysis, synthesis, plans) may use up to Specialist under `light` and Frontier under `balanced`/`heavy`; coordination-only and unrelated work keep the general ceiling.
- Select the cheapest capable model: Specialist for substantive research by default (lookup, verification, comparison); Frontier for difficult, conflicting, or consequential analysis and synthesis; Economy only for mechanical retrieval with no judgment (such as fetching a known page or quote). Medium effort, high for difficult, conflicting, or consequential analysis.
- Verify source access; a stronger model cannot compensate for inaccessible evidence.
