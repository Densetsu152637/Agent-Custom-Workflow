# Research dispatch policy

Manager-only selection procedure: load before resolving a possible research assignment’s eligibility, scoped ceiling, model or effort. Research workers receive the resolved configuration and need the methods core, not these selection algorithms.

## Specialized capability ceiling

Use the [manager ceiling rules](AGENTS.manager.md#capability-ceilings) and [model and effort selection](AGENTS.manager.md#model-and-effort-selection). This section owns research qualification, the scoped maximum, and research-specific model and effort selection.

This table maps task presets only when no explicit research ceiling is set. Explicit research ceilings use the manager’s [literal override mapping](AGENTS.manager.md#capability-ceilings); for example, `research ceiling light` means Economy, not this table’s Specialist task-preset maximum.

| Selected preset | Maximum research tier |
| --------------- | --------------------- |
| light | Specialist |
| balanced | Frontier |
| heavy | Frontier |

- The scoped ceiling applies to substantive research assignments at any difficulty, including factual lookup, source gathering/verification, extraction, comparisons, analysis, synthesis, and research plans. Eligibility follows the assigned research deliverable, not a task label or difficulty.
- Pure research coordination, implementation, and unrelated architecture or code review retain the general ceiling. A manager performing substantive research qualifies; coordination alone does not. Each descendant qualifies on its own assignment; do not pass the research ceiling to unrelated work.
- Verify source access and relevant analytical capability; a stronger model cannot compensate for inaccessible evidence.

## Research model and effort selection

- For each qualifying assignment, select the strongest verified suitable available model within its effective ceiling; do not choose a cheaper model solely because the work is routine. If suitability cannot be verified, use an established capable role and disclose the limitation; do not infer capability from a model's name.
- Use medium effort for ordinary research when the control is available; high for difficult, conflicting, uncertain, or consequential analysis. Do not automatically select maximum effort; honor explicit user restrictions, host controls, and the manager's budget and process limits.
